A cross-platform Qt application for creating, formatting, verifying, and capturing bootable media on Windows, Linux, and macOS.

Status: field-testing preview. The application is feature-ready, but broad physical-device testing and production macOS signing are still outstanding. Back up important data and verify the selected target before writing.

Usage

  1. Connect and select a removable target. Confirm its model and capacity.
  2. Select an image or installer. Rufus++ analyzes the source and enables applicable options.
  3. Review the settings. Choose the layout, filesystem, format, and verification policy where available.
  4. Select Start. Read the destructive-write summary and confirm the exact target.
  5. Wait for completion. Do not disconnect the device while writing or verification is in progress.

Features

  • Image deployment — ISO, raw, compressed, VHD, VHDX, and FFU image analysis and writing.
  • Operating-system media — Windows installation and Windows To Go media, detected Linux persistence, and native macOS installer creation.
  • Disk preparation — MBR and GPT layouts, BIOS and UEFI targets, standalone formatting, and selectable quick or full format.
  • Blank boot media — FreeDOS, user-supplied MS-DOS and bootloaders, bundled GRUB2 and Syslinux, UEFI:NTFS, and offline UEFI Shell media.
  • Capture and inspection — Drive capture to DD, VHD, VHDX, FFU, or UDF ISO where supported, plus read-only device and partition inspection.
  • Verification and safety — MD5, SHA-1, SHA-256, and SHA-512 checksums, post-write verification, bad-block testing, guarded device access, and destructive-write confirmation.
